Pune's Finest Artisanal Patisserie

Cake Caboodle started in 2020 as a passionate home-based cloud bakery, serving custom cakes and confections to Pune. In 2025, we opened our beautiful patisserie at SEASONS MALL, Magarpatta, bringing our artisanal creations closer to our customers.

Our premium patisserie specializes in custom cakes for all occasions - birthdays, anniversaries, festivals, and celebrations. We serve Hadapsar, Kharadi, Magarpatta City, and surrounding Pune areas with handcrafted cakes, artisanal chocolates, and elegant corporate gift hampers.

Whether you need a custom cake for your special celebration, birthday party treats, or corporate gifting solutions, Cake Caboodle delivers excellence. We offer both walk-in services at our Magarpatta patisserie and convenient home delivery throughout Pune via Zomato and Swiggy.
